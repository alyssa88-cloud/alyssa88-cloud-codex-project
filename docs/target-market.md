# Initial Target Market & ICP

## Market

- Primary market: United States
- Primary customer types: apparel brands, apparel wholesalers, and e-commerce sellers
- Minimum target order quantity: 100 units

## Product categories

1. Lingerie / underwear
2. Lingerie sets
3. Shapewear
4. Sleep dresses / nightdresses

## Supplier positioning

The product should emphasize a source-factory / manufacturer positioning, flexible small-batch supply, and the ability to support customers who do not want large minimum order quantities.

## Lead qualification signals

Prioritize companies that:

- Sell women's underwear, lingerie, shapewear, sleepwear, or closely related apparel.
- Operate a recognizable consumer brand, wholesale business, or e-commerce store.
- Have an active website or storefront with current products.
- Show signs of sourcing, expanding product lines, launching new collections, or working with suppliers.
- Can plausibly start with an order around 100 units or otherwise fit a small-batch sourcing model.

Deprioritize or flag for manual review:

- Companies unrelated to apparel.
- Companies with no credible public business presence.
- Leads for which the system cannot establish a reliable product/category match.
- Contact data that cannot be traced to a permitted public or user-provided source.

## Initial lead score

Score from 0-100 using transparent factors:

- Product/category fit: 0-30
- Customer type fit: 0-20
- Evidence of active selling: 0-15
- Small-batch / sourcing fit: 0-15
- Market fit (US): 0-10
- Quality and recency of evidence: 0-10

Do not invent evidence. Unknown information should remain unknown and reduce confidence rather than being guessed.

## Initial outreach positioning

The AI should position the supplier around:

- Lingerie, underwear sets, shapewear, and sleep dresses.
- Factory-direct sourcing.
- Flexible small-batch orders starting around 100 units when commercially applicable.
- Product customization / OEM capability only when actually supported by the supplier.
- Sample-first or quotation-first conversations where appropriate.

All outbound messages must be reviewable by a human before sending in the MVP.
